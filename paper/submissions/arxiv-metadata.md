Title: Hamiltonian cycles on square grids with a central 2 x 2 hole

Author: Jason Roell

Primary category: math.CO

MSC 2020: 05C30, 05C45, 05C85

Abstract: We enumerate undirected Hamiltonian cycles on the 2n by 2n lattice grid with its four central vertices deleted, for 1 <= n <= 10. Two frontier implementations with different connectivity encodings give identical exact counts. Half-board and quarter-board reductions count cycles fixed by square symmetries, yielding geometric orbit counts and exact stabilizer classes. We prove that quarter-turn symmetry is impossible when n is odd. A separate edge-orbit search verifies the symmetry counts through the 8 by 8 case, and additionally verifies quarter-turn and both-axis counts through 12 by 12. The source code, tables, resource measurements, and verification procedures are public. The finite-size comparisons are descriptive and do not establish a new limiting growth constant or exponent.
